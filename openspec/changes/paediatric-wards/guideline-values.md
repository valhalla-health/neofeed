# Paediatric parenteral nutrition: what the guidelines print, 1 month to 18 years

Input for gate 0.5 of `tasks.md`. Nothing here is a proposal for the calculator, and nothing here is
coded. The rule owner and Pp decide each paediatric guard from these rows (`design.md` D5).

- **Read:** 2026-11-21, from the source named in each row: the 14 ESPGHAN/ESPEN/ESPR/CSPEN 2018
  chapters (Clin Nutr 2018;37), the Thai paediatric PN guideline 2564 (public-hearing draft), Mehta
  2017 (SCCM/ASPEN) and the product labels listed at the end.
- **Checked:** the same day, by a second reader who had not seen how the table was made and who
  opened every source again. Of 214 rows, 211 matched the source, 2 were corrected (both are
  corrected below: the Amiparen label, and dosing weight in Mehta 2017) and 1 stayed unread (the
  Addamel N manufacturer label). Units that could be µg or mg were read from the page image.
- **Not yet checked twice:** the section "Found by the second reader" near the end. Each item there
  was read by one reader only and needs a second read before anyone relies on it.
- "Page" is the journal page for ESPGHAN chapters and the printed page for the Thai guideline
  (printed page = PDF page minus 18; appendix pages are PDF pages).
- Where two sources differ, both rows are given and not reconciled.
- The table held a short quotation for every row while it was being checked. They are left out of
  this copy; the recommendation or table number and the page locate each value.

Source keys: **Joosten 2018** (PMID 30078715), **van Goudoever 2018** (30100107), **Lapillonne 2018** (30143306), **Mesotten 2018** (30037708), **Jochum 2018** (30064846), **Mihatsch 2018** (30097365), **Domellöf 2018** (30078716), **Bronsky 2018** (30100105), **Kolaček 2018** (30055869), **Hartman 2018** (30033173), **Puntis 2018** and **Hill 2018** (ESPGHAN organisational and home-PN chapters; PMIDs not checked in this session), **Thai CPG 2564** (public-hearing draft, September 2021; no PMID), **Mehta 2017** (ASPEN/SCCM, DOI 10.1177/0148607117711387; PMID not checked in this session), and product labels named in each row.
