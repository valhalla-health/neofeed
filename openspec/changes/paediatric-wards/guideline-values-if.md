# Intestinal failure, short bowel and IFALD: what the guidelines print

Input for design D7 and tasks group 7 (`tasks.md`), beside `guideline-values.md`. Children and
newborns on PN for intestinal failure, short bowel syndrome or PN-associated liver disease
(cholestasis), including long-term and home PN. Nothing here is a proposal for the calculator, and
nothing here is coded.

- **Read:** 2026-11-21. Sources: ESPGHAN/ESPEN/ESPR/CSPEN 2018 (complications, lipids, home PN,
  organisation, trace minerals, venous access and the nutrient chapters), Modi 2022 and Wales 2014
  (ASPEN), Lacaille 2015 (ESPGHAN, IFALD), Merritt 2017 (NASPGHAN), Norsa 2023 parts 1 and 2 (ESPGHAN,
  short bowel), Wendel 2021 (NASPGHAN, venous access), Diamanti 2022 (SIGENP, teduglutide), the Thai
  guideline 2564 draft, and the Omegaven, SMOFlipid, Gattex and Revestive labels.
- **Checked:** the same day, by a second reader and three sub-readers who opened every source again;
  every ESPGHAN 2018 value was taken from the rendered page image. Of 529 rows, 514 matched and 15
  were corrected. **No clinical number, unit, sign or age band was wrong.** The corrections are
  listed next; the rows below are as first written, so read a corrected row with its correction.
- A row marked [REPORTED] is a figure the guideline reports from a study, not its own
  recommendation. The second reader found six more rows of that kind that carry no mark: glucose
  3.5–7 mg/kg/min in Norsa part 1 (one study), aluminium below 5 µg/kg/day in Wales, and four
  others in the lipid and line-lock tables of Wales and Merritt.
- HTML-read papers (Wales, Merritt, Lacaille, Norsa) are cited by section, box and table, not page.
- The table held a short quotation for every row while it was being checked; they are left out here.
  The full working tables, the second reader's row-by-row verdicts, the passages as printed, what the
  Thai 2565 final changes row by row, and the list of values the table left out are kept in Pp's
  notes (`NeoFeed\Clinical rules\guideline-tables-2026-11-21\`), not in this repository.

## Corrections from the second reader

1. Mesotten R 5.2 (hyperglycaemia from excess glucose): the evidence level is LoE 1−, not LoE 1.
2. Puntis section 2.6 (cycling is safe in stable newborns): LoE 2−, not LoE 2.
3. Riskin, individually tailored PN for children on prolonged PN such as short bowel: it is R 13.2
   (LoE 2, RG B, strong), not R 13.3; R 13.3 is the computerised-prescription recommendation.
4. Hill R 12.7 (single bag for home PN): the recommendation says it "may be recommended"
   (conditional); the row's "preferred" is stronger than the source.
5. to 11. Kolaček venous-access rows (taurolidine R 10.25 and its concentrations, the reported sepsis
   rates, ethanol locks R 10.24, heparin R 10.26 to R 10.28): wording and values are right; the
   recommendation box is on p2384 and the table on p2380, not p2385.
12. Merritt, referral to an intestinal rehabilitation programme: the conditions are alternatives
    (PN longer than 3 months without progress, or high clinical complexity, or worsening IFALD, or
    recurrent sepsis, or thrombosis or loss of venous access), not conditions added to the first.
13. Lacaille, soybean emulsion above 1 g/kg/day and cholestasis: the value is right; the remark that
    the sentence follows an adult study is wrong. The adult figure is a separate sentence elsewhere.
14. Norsa part 2, antidiarrhoeal agents: the recommendation says only that nothing is published in
    children; "may be considered under surveillance" is body text, not the recommendation.
15. Thai guideline, bag and light protection in chapter 11: graded (D1, +), not (A1, ++); the
    (A1, ++) for preterm infants is in chapters 8 and 10.

Also: Wendel prints a tPA concentration (2 mg/2 mL) where a note in section h says none is printed.

## What the second reader adds for design D7

- **Cholestasis threshold.** Modi 2022 prints conjugated bilirubin of 2 mg/dl (34 µmol/L) sustained
  for 2 weeks, without sepsis, after at least 2 weeks of PN, with no > or ≥ sign. No ESPGHAN 2018
  chapter recommends a threshold. The Thai 2564 draft prints none; the 2565 final adds a referral
  criterion on total bilirubin above 6 mg/dL for more than 2 to 4 weeks.
- **Manganese and copper.** ESPGHAN 2018 R 7.18: in cholestasis, measure blood manganese and
  discontinue parenteral manganese. The same chapter's text says removing copper in cholestasis is
  not necessary and may cause deficiency, and that copper status should be monitored. The Thai
  guideline, chapter 11, says copper and manganese may both be withheld for a time in severe
  cholestasis. Wales, Merritt, Lacaille and Norsa say nothing on either.
- **Zinc and sodium with stoma losses.** No source prints a zinc dose for stoma losses; ESPGHAN R 7.9
  asks for more frequent monitoring and Norsa part 1 says a higher parenteral intake prevents
  deficiency. Norsa part 1 gives the urinary sodium target for short bowel (above 20 mmol/L and above
  urinary potassium), and repeats it for neonates with stomas.
- **Lipid.** Only Wales 2014 prints a dose for cholestasis (soybean emulsion ≤ 1 g/kg/d, very low
  evidence, weak). ESPGHAN R 4.18 and Norsa give no figure. No source prints a number of lipid days
  per week.
- **Cycling.** The sources differ on who may be cycled: Norsa from 4 kg, Hartman all but very low
  birth weight infants, Puntis stable newborns, Lacaille not newborns, the Thai 2565 final usually
  from 6 to 12 months.

### Thai paediatric PN guideline B.E. 2564 and product labels: Internal inconsistencies seen in TH2564

1. **Manganese in cholestasis.** Ch. 7 (p. 52): "ควรหยุด" (should stop) for any cholestasis (D2, +). Ch. 11 (p. 86): "อาจพิจารณางด ... ชั่วคราว" (may consider withholding temporarily), only for severe cholestasis, and for copper as well (D2, +).
2. **Copper in cholestasis.** Ch. 11 allows withholding copper; ch. 7 warns that copper deficiency occurs in cholestatic patients given none, and recommends monitoring, not withholding.
3. **Cyclic infusion and cholestasis.** Ch. 3 (p. 25) says cholestasis incidence did not differ between cyclic and continuous (A2, +, refs 17–18); ch. 10 (p. 72–73) says cyclic PN lowers the risk of liver abnormality on long-term PN and cites the same Jensen 2009 paper; ch. 11 recommends cyclic PN (B3, ++).
4. **Lipid range for preterm infants.** Table 3.2 gives a maximum of 3–4 g/kg/day; the Appendix ข summary prints "1–4".
5. **SMOFlipid linoleic acid.** Table 3.1 prints 18.7% of fatty acids; the Appendix ก product table prints 20.
6. **"Long-term".** Definitions: more than 3–4 weeks. Iron monitoring: more than 4 weeks. Fish-oil composite ILE: 4 weeks or more. Vitamins: "several weeks". PNALD: after more than 2 weeks. Home PN: more than 3 months.
7. **Cross-references.** Ch. 2 (p. 11) points to "ตารางที่ 7.1" for amino acids (the table is 2.1); ch. 6 (p. 45) points to "บทที่ 12 ตารางที่ 4", and there is no chapter 12; ch. 7 (p. 49) points to "ตารางที่ 1" (the table is 7.1).
8. **Draft status.** The posted PDF is the public-hearing draft (watermark on every page, footer 19/09/2021); the preface describes a hearing on 29 October 2564, after the footer date.
