# ASPEN: what its documents print on parenteral nutrition for children

Input for gate 0.5 of `tasks.md`, beside `guideline-values.md`. Nothing here is a proposal for the
calculator, and nothing here is coded.

- **Read:** 2026-11-21, from the source named in each row.
- **Checked:** the same day, by a second reader who opened every source again. Of 274 value rows,
  262 matched, 8 were corrected (all for an age band, a section label or a clause; no number, unit
  or sign was wrong; the corrections are applied below) and 4 are "not found" rows with nothing to
  compare. Tables whose bands use < or >, or whose unit could be µg or mg, were read from the
  rendered page.
- **Not read: ASPEN's "Appropriate Dosing for Parenteral Nutrition" (listed 11.2020).** It is behind
  ASPEN's member login. ASPEN's own paediatric nutrient tables in this file therefore come from
  Mirtallo 2004, which ASPEN labels "Archived". Someone with an ASPEN membership needs to supply the
  sheet before an ASPEN dose is taken as current.
- **Not yet checked twice:** "Found by the second reader" at the end, and the companion file
  `guideline-values-aspen-2.md` until its own check is recorded there.
- Rows that the first reader had flagged (a value read through an extraction tool, from a secondary
  paper, or from a table whose cells had run together) were each re-read from the original; the
  flags are removed where the row was confirmed. Wong Vega's "ASPEN" electrolyte column is
  Mirtallo's Table VIII with calcium and magnesium converted at 1 mmol = 2 mEq.
- The table held a short quotation for every row while it was being checked; they are left out here.

## Corrections to the edition notes (second reader)

- Charney 2004 (aluminium) has a published erratum, NCP 2004;19(5):vi; it changes the authorship only.
- ASPEN's Guidelines page links the aluminium statement to the correct article.
- ASPEN's Guidelines page links Corkins 2013 as DOI 10.1177/0884533613475822.
- da Silva 2020's erratum corrects Tables 3 and 5 and the byline. Cober 2021 carries a correction of
  7 December 2021 to one vitamin E figure in its Table 2.
- Vanek 2012's Table 6 rests on Greene HL et al., Am J Clin Nutr 1988;48:1324-1342.
