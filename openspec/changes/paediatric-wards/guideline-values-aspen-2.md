# ASPEN, second set: eight more papers on nutrition support in children

Companion to `guideline-values-aspen.md`. Input for gates 0.5 and 0.5b of `tasks.md`. Nothing here
is a proposal for the calculator, and nothing here is coded.

- **Set A** (read from the PDFs in Pp's library, first page to last): da Silva 2020 (refeeding
  syndrome), Becker 2015 (malnutrition indicators), Modi 2022 (definitions in paediatric intestinal
  failure), Worthington 2021 (PN filters).
- **Set B** (read on the journal site): Wales 2014 (intestinal failure at risk of PNALD), Jesuit
  2010 (hospitalised children with obesity), Corkins 2013 (standards), Modi 2022 again.
- **Read:** 2026-11-21. **Checked:** the same day by a second reader who opened every source again
  and read the table pages from the page image. Of 225 rows, 221 matched and 4 were corrected; all
  four corrections are applied below. No number, unit, sign or age band was wrong against the
  source each row names.
- **The one correction that changes a clinical cell is da Silva's published erratum** (Nutr Clin
  Pract 2020;35(3):584-585, DOI 10.1002/ncp.10491), which the downloaded PDF does not carry: after
  it, Table 5 has no electrolyte criterion for significant refeeding risk. Its text is quoted in the
  Erratum section below. The article's own page on the journal site still shows the uncorrected table.
- Of these eight, only da Silva and Wales print a number that bears on a PN dose. A figure marked as
  a cited study is one the guideline reports, not its own recommendation.
- **Not yet checked twice:** "Found by the second reader" at the end.
- The tables held a short quotation for every row while they were being checked; they are left out here.

## Erratum to da Silva 2020 (second reader)

Found on the journal site. The article page (DOI 10.1002/ncp.10474) carries a "Correction(s) for this article" link to: "Erratum: Joshua S. V. da Silva. ASPEN Consensus Recommendations for Refeeding Syndrome. Nutr Clin Pract. 2020;35(2):178-195", first published 15 April 2020, Nutr Clin Pract 2020;35(3):584-585, DOI 10.1002/ncp.10491. The article's own HTML tables on the journal site still show the original, uncorrected text; the corrected tables are only in the erratum.

What it changes in Table 5 (paediatric), word for word:

- "The Column 2 header should read “3 Risk Criteria Needed,” rather than “3 Risk Categories Needed”".
- "In Column 4 (“Significant Risk: 1 Risk Criterion Needed”), Row 4 (“Abnormal prefeeding serum potassium, phosphorus, or magnesium concentrations”), the statement “Moderately/significantly abnormal or down to 25%–50% below lower limit of normal” should be deleted and this spot should be blank."
- The corrected Table 5 printed in the erratum shows that cell as footnote c, "This cell left intentionally blank", and heads the last column "Significant Risk: 1 Risk Criterion Needed".

What it leaves as it was in Table 5: the row label "Weight loss" with cells "<75% / <50% / <25% of norm for expected weight gain"; the empty mild cell in "Loss of muscle mass"; every z-score, MUAC and energy-intake cell; footnotes a and b.

Consequence for a reader: after the erratum, abnormal prefeeding potassium, phosphorus or magnesium is a mild-risk or moderate-risk criterion only. Table 5 has no electrolyte criterion for significant risk.

Other changes in the same erratum (not Table 5): the byline's last two authors should read "ASPEN Parenteral Nutrition Safety Committee; ASPEN Clinical Practice Committee". Table 3 (adults), electrolyte row: moderate should read "Minimally low levels or normal current levels with recent low levels necessitating minimal or single-dose supplementation"; significant should read "Moderately/significantly low levels or minimally low/normal levels with recent low levels necessitating significant or multiple-dose supplementation" (the original had "and recent low levels" and "minimally low or normal levels").

Erratum or correction notices for the other papers: none on the Wiley pages of Wales 2014, Jesuit 2010 or Corkins 2013 (page text searched for "correct", "erratum", "corrigend", "retract"); none printed in the Becker, Modi or Worthington PDFs.
